# Original Thumbnail Concepts: What Every Miracle Cure in History Did to Your Body

**Rules used for every concept:** 1280×720 (16:9). The character fills the left 40–50%, and the text block sits in the top-right. The **bottom-right 25% stays empty** because the timestamp covers it. The main text is 3 words or fewer, with one toxic-green `#9BE15D` or oxblood `#8C2F39` accent word. Add the text in your editor (Canva, Photoshop and so on), not in the image generator, so the lettering stays sharp.

**Text style for all concepts:** Heavy rounded slab-serif in parchment cream `#F2E6CF` with a thick dark-brown `#3A2418` outline and a soft drop shadow. The accent word is toxic green or oxblood.

---

## 1. "The Cure Killed" (recommended)
- **Visual concept:** The patient smiles, trusting, and raises a glowing green tonic bottle like a toast. On the wall behind, the bottle's shadow forms a large skull.
- **Text overlay:** THE CURE / **KILLED** (with KILLED in toxic green)
- **Mechanism:** Dramatic irony. The patient is happy and the viewer can see the danger. The phrase contradicts itself (a cure that kills), which makes people want to click.
- **Composition:** The patient's face is at the upper-left third and the bottle is raised near the face. The skull shadow fills the center background. Text is top-right. Bottom-right is dark and empty.
- **Image prompt:** Flat 2D cut-paper cartoon patient with a pear-shaped combined head-and-body, wide oval eyes with single highlights, a big trusting grin and mitten hands, wearing a cream hospital gown, raising a small corked bottle of glowing toxic-green liquid like a toast. Behind the patient, the bottle's shadow on the apothecary wall forms a huge ominous skull. Dim 19th-century apothecary with shelves of glass bottles, heavily darkened and softly blurred. Patient on the left 45% of the frame, face in the upper-left third. Top-right and bottom-right areas left empty and dark for text. Strong green under-glow on the face, deep shadows elsewhere. Wobbly dark-brown ink outline, thick cream sticker border around the character, high contrast, bold and readable at small size, 16:9, no text.

## 2. "Glowing Health"
- **Visual concept:** The 1920s businessman beams while sipping radium water. His lower jaw and teeth show through as a glowing green x-ray, so he can't see the damage but we can.
- **Text overlay:** GLOWING / **HEALTH** (with HEALTH in toxic green)
- **Mechanism:** Ironic wordplay combined with an x-ray reveal. The viewer sees something the character can't.
- **Composition:** A tight close-up with the head and shoulders filling the left half. The glowing jaw sits near the center-left, which is the brightest point. Text is top-right.
- **Image prompt:** Flat 2D cut-paper cartoon businessman with a pear-shaped combined head-and-body, slicked hair, pinstriped 1920s suit and a huge delighted smile, sipping from a glowing toxic-green bottle. His jaw and teeth show through the skin as a glowing green x-ray skeleton outline. Dark wood-paneled art deco study behind, blurred and dim. Character on the left half, face large and center-left. Top-right and bottom-right kept empty and dark. Eerie green glow lighting the face from the bottle. Wobbly dark-brown ink outline, thick cream sticker border, high contrast, readable at thumbnail size, 16:9, no text.

## 3. "Pick Your Cure"
- **Visual concept:** The nervous patient stands in front of a shelf holding all six cures, each lit like a game-show prize: a bronze blade, a lancet with a bowl, a mercury jar, an arsenic tin, a pill jar and a glowing radium bottle.
- **Text overlay:** PICK YOUR / **CURE** (with CURE in oxblood)
- **Mechanism:** A choice for the viewer that feels like a game. It invites comments ("I'd take…") and previews the whole list at a glance.
- **Composition:** The patient is on the left third, sweating and pointing hesitantly. The shelf runs across the center to the right in the upper-middle band. Text is above the shelf at the top-right, and the bottom-right is kept dark.
- **Image prompt:** Flat 2D cut-paper cartoon patient with a pear-shaped combined head-and-body, wide nervous oval eyes, a sweat drop and mitten hands, wearing a cream hospital gown, pointing hesitantly toward a wooden apothecary shelf. On the shelf, lit by individual spotlights: a bronze crescent blade, a steel lancet with a pewter bowl, a jar of white powder, a small round tin, a jar of pills and a bottle of glowing toxic-green liquid. Dark apothecary background. Patient on the left third, shelf across the middle-right band. Top-right and bottom-right kept clear and dark. Warm spotlight pools, deep shadows. Wobbly dark-brown ink outline, cream sticker border, high contrast, 16:9, no text.

## 4. "Doctor's Orders"
- **Visual concept:** A tall, thin cartoon doctor smiles far too widely beside an oblivious, cheerful patient. Behind his back, the doctor hides a lancet and a jar of mercury powder.
- **Text overlay:** DOCTOR'S / **ORDERS** (with ORDERS in oxblood)
- **Mechanism:** Dramatic irony plus a sense of betrayal: the person you trust is the threat. It fits the medical-comedy brand from State 2.
- **Composition:** The doctor's tall figure fills the left 40% of the frame, with the smaller patient at the center. The hidden tools are visible to the viewer behind the doctor's back. Text is top-right.
- **Image prompt:** A very tall, thin flat 2D cut-paper cartoon doctor with a long oval face, an unsettlingly wide smile, a dark frock coat and a top hat, hands behind his back secretly holding a steel lancet and a jar of white powder. Beside him stands a short, cheerful pear-shaped cut-paper cartoon patient in a cream hospital gown, smiling and unaware. Dim candlelit 19th-century clinic, blurred. Doctor on the left 40%, patient center-left, hidden tools clearly visible to the viewer. Top-right and bottom-right kept empty and dark. Warm candlelight from below for a sinister look. Wobbly dark-brown ink outline, thick cream sticker borders, high contrast, 16:9, no text.

## 5. "Worth It?"
- **Visual concept:** A split before-and-after of the Victorian woman. On the left she is pale, glowing and delighted, holding an arsenic wafer tin. On the right she is grey and exhausted, her face dotted with brown spots and her eyes horrified.
- **Text overlay:** **WORTH IT?** (with the question mark in toxic green)
- **Mechanism:** A transformation reveal plus a question, which invites the viewer to judge and to click to find out what happened.
- **Composition:** A vertical split through the left two-thirds of the frame: before, then after. The text is stacked in the top-right third, and the bottom-right is kept empty.
- **Image prompt:** A split-panel image of the same flat 2D cut-paper cartoon Victorian woman with a pear-shaped combined head-and-body, hair in a tidy bun and a high-collared mustard dress. Left panel: pale porcelain skin, sparkles, a delighted smile, holding a small round tin of wafers. Right panel: grey, exhausted skin dotted with small brown spots, dark circles and a horrified open-mouthed expression. A torn-paper divider between the panels, both occupying the left two-thirds of the frame. A foggy gaslit Victorian parlour background, dim and blurred. Top-right and bottom-right kept empty and dark. Soft greenish gaslight. Wobbly dark-brown ink outline, cream sticker border, high contrast, 16:9, no text.
