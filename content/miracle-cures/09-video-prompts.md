# Video Prompts (60): What Every Miracle Cure in History Did to Your Body

**How to use:** These are image-to-video prompts. Upload Image N from `08-image-prompts.md` as the first frame, then paste Video N.
**Clip length:** Generate 5 s or 10 s clips, as noted. Where the narration runs longer, slow the clip down or hold its last frame in editing.
**Add to every prompt (style lock):** *Keep the exact character designs, colors and cut-paper style of the source image. Motion has a slightly choppy stop-motion puppet feel, the background shows gentle diorama parallax, and the camera moves smoothly. No morphing, no new characters, no text changes, no gore.*
**Negative prompt:** realistic humans, 3D render, extra limbs, face distortion, flicker, warping text, blood spray, graphic injury.
